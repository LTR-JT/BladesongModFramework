## Bladesong Mod Framework 0.1.18

- Added a Sword Parts minimize button beside the catalogue resize control. The button follows the panel as it resizes.
- Minimizing hides the catalogue and Polish Level. Select the bottom **+ Sword Parts** tab to reopen them. Part tooltips stay available above the tab.
- Added a **Hide tang** checkbox to Options. It remembers your preference and changes visibility without editing sword saves.
